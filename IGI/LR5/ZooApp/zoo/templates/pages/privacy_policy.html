Privacy Policy — Greenfield City Zoo

Effective date: September 12, 2025

1. Introduction

Greenfield City Zoo ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting visitors’ and supporters’ personal data. This Privacy Policy describes what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, with whom we share it, how long we retain it, and the rights you have in relation to your personal data.

2. Scope

This Policy applies to personal data we collect via our website, ticketing systems, membership programs, on-site forms, CCTV used for safety, volunteer or employment applications, and other interactions with the Zoo.

3. What data we collect

We collect personal data necessary to provide services, ensure safety, and manage operations. Examples include:

  • Identity & account data: name, username
  • Contact data: email address, phone number
  • Transaction data: ticket purchases, donations, membership information
  • Health & accessibility data: voluntarily provided information required for safe handling or animal encounters
  • Technical data: IP address, device/browser data for site performance and fraud prevention
  • Visual records: CCTV footage for site security (retained for limited periods)

4. Why we collect data & lawful basis

We process data for purposes including:

  • Providing ticketing, membership, and donation services (contractual necessity).
  • Protecting visitor safety and site security (legitimate interests / compliance with law).
  • Communicating news, events and offers when you consent to marketing (consent).
  • Meeting statutory obligations (e.g., tax and accounting).

5. Who we share data with

We may share personal data with:

  • Payment processors and banks to complete transactions.
  • Third-party service providers (ticketing platforms, hosting providers) under contract and confidentiality obligations.
  • Law enforcement or regulators where required by law or to protect public safety.
  • Research partners for protected, de-identified conservation research (only when consented or legally permitted).

6. Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described above and to meet legal and business record requirements. Typical retention periods: transactional records (7 years for accounting), membership records (duration of membership + 2 years), CCTV footage (30–90 days unless needed for an incident).

7. Contact & changes

Questions or complaints: privacy@greenfieldzoo.example or postal mail to Greenfield City Zoo, Attn: Privacy Officer, 123 Meadow Lane, Greenfield.

We may update this Policy — the effective date above indicates when it was last revised.
